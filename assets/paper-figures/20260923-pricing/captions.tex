\newcommand{\SafeOTConceptCaption}{One price rule across safe-RL regimes. SafeOT-Dual combines a clipped, filtered graph-solved price with realized-cost feedback. Under the endpoint assumptions, the graph problem approaches a constrained LP as $\varepsilon\to0$; at large $\varepsilon$, a positive limiting information-projection multiplier yields a clipped active-set price. Clipping bounds the graph contribution in both regimes. At cap $c=0$, fixed $\beta$ gives a fixed penalty and adaptive $\beta$ gives a Lagrangian rule. These regimes do not imply exact recovery of external constrained-policy algorithms. Miniatures are partial schematic graphs on shared support, not measured or complete balanced-flow solutions.}
\newcommand{\SafeOTFrameworkCaption}{On-policy SafeOT-Dual. Each rollout supplies an empirical graph, reward/cost GAE advantages and episode-cost feedback. Joint graph pricing uses discounted flow balance, per-step soft budgets $b_k=B_k/L$ and hard-edge support to obtain $\lambda^\star$. Only prices transfer: after clipping and filtering, the graph price combines with feedback integrator $\beta$ in a normalized priced-advantage PPO/TRPO step. GAE follows a separate branch; the new policy generates the next rollout. Graph feasibility alone does not certify policy safety. The separate off-policy executor is not shown.}
