SafeOT converts rollout structure into safety prices for a standard policy update. The empirical flow $\widehat F$ supplies the reference for entropic projection, with flow balance, soft budgets $b_k$, and hard exclusions $H$. Here $\mathcal L$ denotes the flow-balance operator, $\varepsilon$ the regularization strength, $c$ the clipping cap, $\eta$ the feedback step, and $B_k$ the episode budget. GAE advantages enter the priced advantage directly; realized costs update $\beta_k$. The target flow $F^*$ is never executed.
