SafeOT-Dual adds joint flow pricing to a standard policy update. Gray modules are standard RL; colored modules build and solve the budgeted graph, then combine its clipped, filtered dual prices with observed-cost feedback. Width encodes visits: projection shifts flow away from the costly route while excluding hard edges. Only prices enter PPO/TRPO; the target flow is not executed. The feedback equation illustrates projected ascent; implementations may use an adaptive optimizer.
