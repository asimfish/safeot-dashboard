# Figure estimands and source boundaries

Figure 1B uses the same 600-row all-task curve source as Figure 3(c). Lines are medians across per-task seed means. The shaded excess above 1 is descriptive median-curve area, not the benchmark V statistic. Primary V annotations are separately reported: SafePO original five-seed task-median ratio 0.3741627178 with task-bootstrap interval [0.2019631304, 0.5140837543]; Bullet ratio of suite means 0.2212261375 with interval [0.1308798837, 0.4550476109]. Their counts are 10/10 and 6/6. Input hashes and exact supplied values are in primary_V_summaries.json.

Figure 3(a,b) retains all 31 original a41 producer estimates and intervals. In that broader SafePO B25 snapshot the TRPO-Lag task-median ratio is 0.336 [0.223, 0.515]; Bullet's task-median ratio is 0.3010476368 [0.1827143685, 0.5969807690]. These differ from the primary headline estimators; do not present them as interchangeable. Any switch of cohort/estimator requires a new producer CSV and aligned counts. The log axis is reversed for reading direction only.

Forest return difference is ours minus comparator, normalized per task by max(abs(comparator mean return), observed return range). Forest intervals are 95% task/within-task-seed bootstrap intervals. Warm is the separate original five-seed control with task-only bootstrap: its V is retained, but its task-win count and return were not supplied (n/r). Dashes mean a comparison was not run in that suite, not zero.

Figure 3(c): median and IQR across task seed means, not seed confidence intervals. Only SafeOT-Dual/TRPO-Lag IQR bands are drawn. The source provides all four methods' IQRs unchanged. Per-task return is divided by the maximum seed-mean return over the four methods and all epochs; this differs from the forest normalizer. SafePO uses 100 progress points, Bullet 50. No smoothing or interpolation is introduced. Bullet source metrics are the producer's common rolling-50 training metrics. Source cohort: SafePO frozen 534-run B25 snapshot; plotted methods have 75/75/60/73 runs and 2–10 seeds per cell. Bullet has 5 seeds per cell, 30 per method. The 16-task appendix retains every provided per-task paired trace.

The Bullet dagger means final evaluation task-mean cost > B=10, not a newly chosen threshold for “far above”. It holds on 4/6 TRPO-Lag tasks and 5/6 PPO-Lag tasks. These are task-level seed means, not per-seed feasibility proportions. All twelve final-cost means and their original seed intervals are copied in bullet_final_cost_means.csv; the exact derivation and source hash are in dagger_cost_receipt.json. Lower training-time V does not assert final policy feasibility.
