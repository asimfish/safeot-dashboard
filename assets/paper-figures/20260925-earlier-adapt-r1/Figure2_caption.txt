SafeOT-Dual transfers joint budget prices from a flow projection to a policy update. Rollouts provide empirical flows, advantages and realized costs. Soft budgets and excluded hard edges constrain one projection. Clipped, filtered dual prices combine with observed-cost feedback to price the PPO/TRPO advantage. Only prices reach the actor; the target flow is not executed. The feedback equation shows projected ascent; the implementation may use an adaptive optimizer.
