All-task training curves. Left: all ten SafePO tasks, B = 25, 2M steps. Right: all six Bullet tasks, B = 10, 1M steps. The x axis is percent of each suite’s training budget. At each epoch, the producer first averages paired reward and cost over the seeds within each task/method, then reports the median and 25th–75th percentiles across tasks. Shading is task IQR, not a seed confidence interval. Cost is divided by B; the dashed line is 1. The Bullet cost axis is symmetric-log, linear up to 1. Return is divided by the maximum seed-mean return among the four shown methods and all training epochs of that task; this is different from the forest’s final-return-difference normalizer. The SafePO curve snapshot contains 534 admitted B25 runs across its full method cohort; the four displayed methods contribute 75/75/60/73 runs, with 2–10 seeds per cell. Bullet contributes five seeds per cell, 30 per displayed method. The producer’s native training grids are retained without smoothing or interpolation by the drawing script. Bullet source metrics use the producer’s common rolling-50 training metric.
