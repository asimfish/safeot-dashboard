\textbf{Graph prices guide a policy update.} Each epoch, a rollout supplies graph estimates, ordinary reward/cost GAE advantages and observed episode costs (Algorithm~\ref{alg:safeot-dual}, lines 4--8). The upper band constructs k-means cells, transition mass $\hat F$, reward-advantage means $A$ and edge costs $C_k$, then solves Eq.~\eqref{eq:proj} subject to discounted balance, soft budgets $b_k=B_k/L$ and hard-edge removal (red dashed cross). The displayed objective abbreviates $\mathrm{KL}(F\|\hat F)$. Only the clipped and exponentially filtered dual prices $\bar\lambda_k^\star$ cross from graph space into policy space; the policy never executes $F^\star$. In the lower band, feedback uses actual observed episode-cost error (Eq.~\eqref{eq:offset-free}); it is added to the graph contribution to give $\lambda_k$. Independent GAE advantages and these prices define Eq.~\eqref{eq:policy-price-interface}, which is passed to PPO/TRPO. The recurrence is the schematic projected update; SafePO uses Adam. Graph feasibility and graph-support removal alone do not guarantee policy budget feasibility or safe actions. Solver internals are deferred to the appendix.
