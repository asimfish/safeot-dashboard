\textbf{One price rule, two views.} SafeOT-Dual solves one entropy-regularized graph allocation with joint soft-cost budgets $b_k$, discounted flow balance and a KL reference. A dashed red cross denotes a hard-event edge removed from graph support ($F_{ij}=0$); hard events are not assigned a finite soft-budget price. The graph dual prices are clipped at $c$, filtered and added to feedback on the actual policy's observed episode-cost error $\bar J_k-B_k$. The resulting prices enter the normalized actor advantage. On the left, $c=0$ removes the graph contribution: frozen feedback gives fixed penalties and adaptive feedback gives a Lagrangian controller setting. On the right, the shaded rectangle depicts the two displayed graph-cost budgets, not the entire feasible-flow set. Under the assumptions of Proposition~\ref{prop:endpoints}, $\varepsilon\to0$ gives LP dual accumulation points; at $\varepsilon\to\infty$, a positive limiting scaled multiplier causes a finite clip to saturate. An off state additionally requires eventual inactivity or a vanishing unscaled multiplier. These are price-family settings and conditional limits, not equivalences to external algorithms or guarantees for learned execution. $b_k$ is the graph-rate budget; $B_k$ is the episode budget.
